# Stage 4(c): The Result We Trust Least

In this project, **the result we trust least as an indication of future achievable investment performance is the Stage 1 tangency portfolio's in-sample annualized Sharpe ratio of 2.197.** Our concern is its interpretation rather than the calculation itself: the portfolio weights were selected and their performance evaluated using the same historical observations. The optimization process may exploit incidental patterns in the sample, making the in-sample performance overly optimistic. Meanwhile, estimation errors in expected returns and covariances can translate into extreme positions. The original tangency portfolio has gross exposure of 5.389 times net asset value; shortening the estimation window from 108 to 84 months raises this to 8.812 times, demonstrating sensitivity to the sample window. Although the portfolio earned a cumulative return of 40.78% in the 2026 tracking period through October 6, that result comes from a retrospectively specified research design. It also excludes full financing, stock-borrowing, and trading costs, and does not simulate forced liquidation under margin constraints. We therefore interpret the high in-sample Sharpe ratio as an optimization outcome within historical data, rather than established evidence of persistent investment skill. Before allocating actual capital on this basis, further out-of-sample validation is needed under strategy rules fixed in advance, implementable position constraints, and comprehensive cost assumptions.
